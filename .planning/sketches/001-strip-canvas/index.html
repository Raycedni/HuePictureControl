What to look for
  • The 40px height makes ticks legible but pushes selection chrome onto outline rather than fill.
  • Narrow zones (like the 18-LED purple slice) disappear textually — discoverable only via selection.
  • The handle visual matches the GSD `handle` primitive — same drag affordance can be reused for boundary handles in any future timeline UI.
  • Sidebar carries all per-channel detail (name + LED range + delete). Strip stays clean.

What to look for
  • Inline name + LED range is readable above ~80px wide; the narrow 6% zone clips to "Ch 3" and the range — still discoverable, no sidebar trip.
  • Strip feels primary; tradeoff is vertical real-estate. Two devices fit comfortably in the slot, four might force scrolling.
  • Boundary handles sit above the strip — easier to grab when the bar is tall.
  • Closest analog to existing project bar styling (rounded 8px, glass surface).

What to look for
  • Unassigned LED span on the Lamp Strip is visible immediately — useful when CONTEXT.md says paint may leave gaps after deletion.
  • Floating boundary pill is friendly to grab but adds a small visual element to track per boundary.
  • Rail underneath the zones means zones can be slightly raised on hover, giving the paint UI a more "physical" feel.
  • Risk: zones-as-tiles starts to feel like grouped buttons rather than a continuous range — may obscure the per-LED nature.
