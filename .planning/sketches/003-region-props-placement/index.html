What to look for
  • Popover lives on the canvas itself — the user's attention stays where the region is.
  • Closes on outside click, on region deselect, on a new region selection.
  • Risk: at the bottom-right corner of the canvas the popover may collide with edges. Needs auto-flip logic (above/below/left/right of the region).
  • Risk: popover obscures other regions or the video preview underneath. The user may need to drag the popover or close it to inspect the underlying frame.
  • LightPanel "Assignments" section can be removed/repurposed — orientation lives on the canvas. CONTEXT.md D-19 explicitly leaves this open.

  • Persistent drawer — no layout shift when selecting/deselecting regions, just content change.
  • Plenty of room for future region-level metadata (axis hint visualizer, brightness multiplier, etc).
